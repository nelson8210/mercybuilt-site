The foundation

Built by mercy, not ego.

Mercy Built began with a personal faith journey: baptism, surrender, and the daily work of becoming a better Christian man. The name is a reminder that strength does not start with pride. It starts with God meeting us in our weakness and rebuilding us by His mercy.

This is apparel for people still being formed. For training hard, leading humbly, falling short, getting back up, and continuing to walk with Christ.
